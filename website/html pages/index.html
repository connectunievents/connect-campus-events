Features

Everything you need,
nothing you don't.

Built around how campus life actually works — not how commercial event platforms think it does.

🎟
Digital QR tickets

Register for an event and instantly get a QR code in your inbox. No printing, no booths, no chaos at the door.

🔒
University-only access

Private events stay private. Only students with a verified university email can see and join internal events.

📊
Organizer dashboard

See who registered, scan attendees at the door, and get a post-event summary — all in one place.

🔔
Smart notifications

Automatic reminders before events and instant waitlist alerts when a spot opens up.

🌐
Cross-university discovery

Find public events from other universities too. Great talks don't stop at your campus gates.

✅
Admin approval flow

Every event is reviewed before going live. Admins keep quality high and the platform trustworthy.

💳 Payments

Pay the way
Lebanon pays.

No international cards, no friction. Paid events on Connect are settled through the payment methods Lebanese students already use every day — Wish and OMT.
