🔒 Privacy Policy

How Connect handles your data.

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Connect is a university events platform built to help students discover events, register with QR-code tickets, and connect event organizers with sponsors. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it's protected.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (stored securely, never in plain text)
  • University affiliation — determined automatically from your email domain, used to grant access to private, university-only events
  • Event activity — events you register for, your registration status, and check-in records for ticketing purposes
  • Sponsor application details — if you apply to become a sponsor, we collect your business name, contact information, and supporting details submitted through the application form

How we use your information

We use your information only to operate the platform: to verify your university status for access to private events, to generate and validate your event tickets, to let organizers manage registrations, and to review sponsor applications before they go live. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties beyond what's needed to run the platform.

Payments

Paid events are processed through Wish Money or OMT. Connect does not store your card or payment account details — payment is handled directly through these external providers, and we only retain a record of whether a payment was completed for your registration.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored using Supabase, a hosted PostgreSQL database provider. Passwords are hashed and never stored in readable form. Access to private, university-scoped events is enforced at the database level, meaning your data is protected by rules that apply regardless of how a request reaches our systems — not just by what the website's interface shows you.

Your choices

You can update your profile information at any time once logged in. If you'd like your account or data removed, contact us using the details below and we'll process your request.
