The terms behind using Connect.

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

These terms govern your use of Connect, a university events platform that helps students discover and register for events, and connects event organizers with sponsors. By creating an account, you agree to the terms below.

Who can use Connect

Connect is open to two types of users:

  • General users — anyone with a valid email address can browse and register for public events
  • Verified students — users who sign up with a recognized university email (LAU, AUB, or NDU) and confirm it receive access to private, university-only events in addition to public ones

You're responsible for keeping your account credentials secure and for all activity that happens under your account.

Event registration

Registering for an event through Connect reserves your spot, subject to the event's stated capacity. Some events may require payment via Wish Money or OMT before registration is confirmed. Each confirmed registration generates a QR code ticket used for check-in at the event. Cancellation and refund policies are set by individual event organizers, not by Connect directly.

Private, university-only events

Access to private events is limited to verified students of the matching university. Attempting to access private events you're not eligible for, or misrepresenting your university affiliation to gain access, is a violation of these terms.

Sponsors

Businesses may apply to become a sponsor through the sponsor application form. All applications are reviewed before approval, and Connect reserves the right to accept or decline any application. Approved sponsors are expected to provide accurate business information.

Acceptable use

You agree not to misuse the platform — including submitting false information, attempting to bypass access controls, or using the platform to harass or spam other users.

Changes to these terms

As Connect continues to develop, these terms may be updated. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted means you accept the revised terms.
